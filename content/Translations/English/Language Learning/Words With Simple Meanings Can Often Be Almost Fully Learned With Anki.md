## Claim

Words with simple, stable meanings can often be learned almost completely through Anki because their initial connotation is already close to their full connotation.

## Why It Matters

This marks an important exception to Anki's context problem. Clear, concrete terms need less contextual enrichment than broad, abstract, register-sensitive, or polysemous words.
