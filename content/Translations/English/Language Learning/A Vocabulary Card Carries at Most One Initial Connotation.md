## Claim

A vocabulary card should carry at most one initial connotation: enough meaning to make the word recognizable, but not an attempt to encode its complete semantic range.

## Why It Matters

One clear first association keeps retrieval simple and leaves nuance, register, collocations, and alternative meanings to later encounters in immersion.
