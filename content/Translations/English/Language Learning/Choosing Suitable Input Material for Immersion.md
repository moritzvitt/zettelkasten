## Claim

Suitable immersion material is interesting enough to hold attention and difficult enough to create learning without making meaning collapse.

## Why It Matters

Material should invite effortless attention while remaining somewhat above the learner's current level. Interest sustains contact; partial comprehensibility lets patterns become learnable.
