## Claim

Functional fluency means: learn nothing unnecessary; become truly capable only in the contexts that matter.

The point is not to master every rare word as early as possible. What matters is moving confidently in the parts of a cultural information network in which you actually live, work, learn, or build relationships.

Specialized vocabulary becomes important only when you enter the corresponding context. Someone who plays soccer in Japan will eventually need a word like "bicycle kick." Someone with no spaceflight context does not need to preemptively learn spaceflight vocabulary.

At the same time, broad contact with the language develops a general language competence that lets you open up new subdomains later.

You may not already know every technical term for spaceflight, but you have the linguistic and cultural foundation to acquire them efficiently when needed.

[^clipping-what-is-language-acquisition]: From the clipping: What is Language Acquisition?; original source: University of Sheffield, What is Language Acquisition; extracted source page: Sheffield What is Language Acquisition.

## Why It Matters

This note clarifies how languages can remain accessible or be reactivated over time without keeping every ability permanently at peak performance.
