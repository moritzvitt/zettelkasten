## Claim

Functional fluency means being fluent in the areas of a language, or cultural forms of communication, that you need for your life.

Learning a language to perfection is impressive, but can also waste time unless it is a hobby or time is abundant. Strictly speaking, native speakers are also only functionally fluent. The initial goal is therefore functional fluency.

Multilingualism, however, is about reactivating languages quickly. Learning beyond functional fluency is not wasted energy: it lets you become philosophical with friends and creates deeper reserves for later reactivation.

You must accept some insecurity when speaking or learning. A language need not be learned "perfectly," whatever that would mean. You could study until you spoke better than some native speakers and still not speak perfectly.

## Why It Matters

This clarifies how languages can remain accessible or be reactivated over time without keeping every ability permanently at peak performance.
