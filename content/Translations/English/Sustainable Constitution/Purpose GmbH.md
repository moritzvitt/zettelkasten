## Claim

The Purpose GmbH attempts to structure company ownership so that control and responsibility are preserved while the free extraction of company assets is limited.

Its core is steward ownership: people can run and develop a company without treating it primarily as a wealth-generating machine. This makes the form interesting for an economic order that does not merely demand sustainability morally, but makes it structurally more likely.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
