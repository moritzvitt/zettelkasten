## Learning Japanese
I learn languages in a [[zettelkasten/zettel/immersion-and-language-acquisition/immersion/best-method,-learning-with-stories-(optimal-immersion-loop)/thematic-immersion-optimizes-re-encounter-density|"thematic" immersion]] approach, centered around topics. That way, it's easier to acquire vocabulary, since you are more likely to encounter the same or similar words, if you stay in a thematic bubble.
