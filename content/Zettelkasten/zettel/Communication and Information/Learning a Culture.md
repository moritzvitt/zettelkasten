# Language as Participation in an Information Network

## Claim

It makes little sense to learn a language in isolation from the communicative forms of a culture. Anyone who wants to move confidently within a cultural space must learn to orient themselves within its whole information network, not only linguistically but also pragmatically, socially, and culturally.

The goal is not merely to "know the language," but to be able to move within the communicative modes of a culture.
