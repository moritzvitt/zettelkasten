# Culture as an Information Network

## Claim

A culture is an information network with established rules and conventions. Culture connects different means of communication and institutions in order to organise itself.

Means of communication:

- language
- body language
- forms of politeness
- rituals

Institutions:

- social roles
- typical situations
- rituals such as forms of politeness

## Why It Matters

This note clarifies one building block for understanding how language, culture, institutions, and media work together as information networks.
