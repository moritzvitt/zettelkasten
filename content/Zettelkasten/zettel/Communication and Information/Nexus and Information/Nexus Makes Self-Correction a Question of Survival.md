# Nexus Makes Self-Correction a Question of Survival

## Claim

For *Nexus*, self-correction is a question of survival for information networks. Because humans and institutions are fallible, every powerful network needs mechanisms that make errors visible and force correction.

Such mechanisms can include scientific peer review, a free press, independent courts, constitutional amendments, opposition, transparency obligations, or technical audits. Without them, a network becomes vulnerable to dogma, corruption, and abuse of power.

## Why It Matters

The most important question for an information system is not only: "How quickly does it spread information?" It is also: "How does it notice that it is wrong?"
