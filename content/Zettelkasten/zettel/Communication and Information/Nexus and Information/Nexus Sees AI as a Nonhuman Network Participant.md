# Nexus Sees AI as a Nonhuman Network Participant

## Claim

In *Nexus*, AI is not just a faster medium. It becomes a nonhuman participant in the information network: it can generate content, make decisions, sort people, influence conversations, and circulate new ideas.[^source-notes]

This distinguishes AI from earlier media such as print, radio, or television. Those media distributed human content; AI can itself become part of the production and steering of information.

## Why It Matters

The political question shifts from "Which information do humans distribute?" to "Which nonhuman systems generate, filter, and prioritize the information through which humans understand their world?"
