# Nexus Warns Against Automated Destruction of Trust

## Claim

*Nexus* warns that AI can destroy trust in an automated way. When texts, images, voices, messages, profiles, and conversation partners can be artificially generated at mass scale, the distinction between human and machine communication becomes politically decisive.

The problem is not only misinformation. The deeper risk is an environment in which people no longer know whom or what they are encountering at all.

## Why It Matters

Democracy needs shared reality and trust in verifiable procedures. When automated systems undermine these foundations faster than institutions can repair them, self-correction itself is damaged.
