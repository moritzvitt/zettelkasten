# Information Connects More Than It Represents

## Claim

For Harari, information is not primarily an accurate representation of reality. Information is above all something that connects: people, institutions, memories, commands, myths, databases, and machines.

A piece of information can be true, but it does not have to be true in order to be effective. False or fictional information can also coordinate people, stabilize power, or trigger action.

Information is therefore a medium through which information networks arise. It circulates between nodes and organizes who is connected, what is remembered, which rules apply, and which actions become likely.

## Why It Matters

This shifts the question from "Is this information true?" to "What does this information do in the network?" Truth remains important, but it is not the only social function of information.
