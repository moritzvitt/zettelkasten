# Nexus Sees Stories as Connection Technology

## Claim

Early information technologies include stories and documents.

*Nexus* treats stories, myths, and shared fictions as connection technologies. They allow strangers to understand themselves as part of the same order and to act together.

The point is not that stories are always true. Their historical leverage lies in their ability to organize trust, obedience, belonging, and cooperation beyond small groups.

## Why It Matters

Anyone who understands information only as a representation of reality misses its social function. Societies are connected not only by facts, but also by shared narratives about what matters, who belongs, and which order one obeys.
