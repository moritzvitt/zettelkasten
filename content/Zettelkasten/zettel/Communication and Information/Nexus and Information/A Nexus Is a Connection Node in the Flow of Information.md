# A Nexus Is a Connection Node in the Flow of Information

## Claim

A nexus is a connection point where information converges, is ordered, and continues to have effects. It is not merely a store, but a node that creates relationships between people, documents, institutions, decisions, and technologies.[^source-notes]

In Harari's book, "nexus" is therefore an image for the power of information networks: whoever controls the nodes and connections influences what a society sees, believes, remembers, and decides.

## Why It Matters

The concept shifts attention away from individual pieces of information and toward the architecture of their connection. What matters is not only which information exists, but where it lands, who processes it, and which chains of action it triggers.
