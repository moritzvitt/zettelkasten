# Nexus Separates Information From Truth

## Claim

In *Nexus*, information is not the same as truth. Information can be true, false, useful, manipulative, or meaning-making; its social effect often arises from the way it connects and coordinates people.

This makes information dangerously ambiguous. A network can become smarter through truth, but it can also become more stable and powerful through fictions, propaganda, or shared delusions.

## Why It Matters

The naive hope that "more information leads to more truth" is not enough. What matters is how a network checks, sorts, rewards, and corrects information.
