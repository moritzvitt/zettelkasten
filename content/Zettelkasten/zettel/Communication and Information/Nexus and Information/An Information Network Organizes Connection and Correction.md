# An Information Network Organizes Connection and Correction

## Claim

An information network is a system of nodes, channels, rules, and stores through which information circulates and becomes effective. It includes people, books, archives, bureaucracies, media, courts, platforms, algorithms, and AI systems.[^source-notes]

For Harari, a good information network is not simply the network with the most data. It is a network that organizes connection, decision, and self-correction in such a way that it is not completely captured by error, myth, or power interests.

## Why It Matters

Information networks shape not only opinions, but political orders. A democracy, a bureaucracy, a religion, or an AI platform differs according to how information flows, who is allowed to check it, and which nodes make decisions.
