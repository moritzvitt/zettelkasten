## Claim

Redundancy is allowed when every retrieval remains simple. Two cards may overlap if each tests a distinct, easy access path.

## Why It Matters

Avoiding all duplication can produce overloaded cards. Limited redundancy is useful when it keeps individual prompts clear and cheap.
