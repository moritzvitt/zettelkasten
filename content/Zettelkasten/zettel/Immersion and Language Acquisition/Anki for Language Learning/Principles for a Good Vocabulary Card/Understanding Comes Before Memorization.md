## Claim

Understanding comes before memorization. Spaced repetition should begin only after a rough mental model of the material exists.

## Why It Matters

SRS can stabilize an understood idea, but it cannot reliably create understanding from an opaque prompt. Memorizing first risks preserving confusion.
