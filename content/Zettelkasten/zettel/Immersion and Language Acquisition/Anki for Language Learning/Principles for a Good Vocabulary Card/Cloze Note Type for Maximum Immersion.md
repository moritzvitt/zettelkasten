## Claim

A cloze note can isolate a small retrieval target inside natural language while keeping the learner close to material encountered in immersion.

The surrounding sentence supplies limited context, but the deletion keeps the review focused. The card should remain quick enough that the context supports retrieval rather than becoming a passage to analyze.

## Why It Matters

Cloze cards can connect SRS directly to real input while respecting the principle of minimal information.
