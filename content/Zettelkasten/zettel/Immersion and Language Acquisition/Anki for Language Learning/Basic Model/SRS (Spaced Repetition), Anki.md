# SRS (Spaced Repetition), Anki

## Claim

Spaced repetition schedules reviews near the point at which a memory would otherwise become inaccessible. Anki is a practical implementation of this principle.

For language learning, its role is not to contain the language, but to keep selected words and weak retrieval points available long enough for immersion to reinforce them.

## Why It Matters

SRS is most useful when it supports later encounters with real language instead of becoming a substitute for them.
