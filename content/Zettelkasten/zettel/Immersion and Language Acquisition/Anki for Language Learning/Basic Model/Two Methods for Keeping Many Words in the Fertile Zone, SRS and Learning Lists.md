# Two Methods for Keeping Many Words in the Fertile Zone, SRS and Learning Lists

## Claim

Spaced repetition is ideally suited for keeping many words in the fertile zone for a longer time.

> [!Fertile Zone]
> The time shortly after learning a word is especially fertile, while the word is still fresh. Immersion can then fill the entry with nuance, sound, register, collocations, and real situations. ==Anki tries to keep many words close enough to this fertile zone at the same time.==

Learning lists are the second method: they make words from a specific medium findable again and increase the chance of nearby, thematically fitting re-encounters, without immediately throwing every word into the long-term review stream.

## Why It Matters

Anki does not mean that words are perfectly learned. It prepares the ground so later encounters in input can actually take hold. The real learning happens when a freshly created retrieval point becomes alive and meaningful through immersion.
