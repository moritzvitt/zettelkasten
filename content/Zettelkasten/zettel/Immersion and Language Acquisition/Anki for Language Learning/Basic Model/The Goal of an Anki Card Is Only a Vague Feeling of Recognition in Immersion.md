# The Goal of an Anki Card Is Only a Vague Feeling of Recognition in Immersion

## Claim

The goal of a language-learning Anki card is recognition in input, in immersion: the brief feeling, "Ah, I know this." The review need not achieve more.[^source-notes] ^8772ee

Visible context must not turn into prolonged study of meaning. A sentence may briefly help when the target word does not appear immediately, but the focus remains rapid retrieval. Context provides at most an initial connotation. ^2b7040

Dwelling on a card, analyzing connotations, or trying to illuminate the whole word works against the card's function. Anki should open initial access, not fully teach the meaning. The weighting of meanings belongs in real immersion. ^f96cd5

## Why It Matters

This boundary protects against Anki perfectionism. A card is good enough when it makes the word recognizable at the next encounter with input. ^9e98fa

[^source-notes]: Source notes: Anki als Sprachlernwerkzeug; Anki bereitet fruchtbare Begegnungen vor.
