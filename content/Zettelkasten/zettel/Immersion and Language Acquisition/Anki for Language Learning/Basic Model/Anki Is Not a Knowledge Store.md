# Anki Is Not a Knowledge Store

## Claim

In your language-learning system, Anki is not where knowledge lives. It is a retrieval tool that prepares words and weak memory points so they can be recognized in input: listening and reading.[^source-notes]

Anki words can sometimes become directly usable in output, especially when their meaning is clear and stable. For words with many connotations, registers, or typical collocations, productive confidence arises only after repeated encounters in real input and personal use.

## Why It Matters

This prevents card maintenance from being confused with learning. Language ability emerges through transfer from cards into real contexts. A card succeeds when it makes later encounters more fertile.
