# The Role of SRS; Preparing Immersion

## Claim

With vocabulary, Anki first creates a mental dictionary entry: an address in the mind that makes a word recognizable the next time you encounter it.

Important: this is only an entry. Definitions, example sentences, synonyms, and the rest are not there yet; they have to be filled in through immersion.

## Why It Matters

The actual learning of the word happens afterwards in input. Every encounter in reading or listening refines the entry through sound, context, register, and collocations. Anki gives the word an address; immersion fills it with meaning.
