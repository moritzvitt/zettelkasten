# Anki Costs Time and Attention

## Claim

Anki costs time not only when cards are created. ==Every card creates future review time and therefore a small, lasting obligation.==

Every Anki card is like another ball thrown into the air while juggling.[^source-notes]

Anki knowledge must therefore be curated continuously: suspend, delete, merge, reformulate, or deprioritize cards once they no longer support the learning loop. ==An Anki collection is not an archive, but a stream of limited attention.==

## Why It Matters

Without curation, the collection grows faster than real learning capacity. Anki then consumes time instead of preparing contact with real input. Good maintenance protects review time, motivation, and immersion time.
