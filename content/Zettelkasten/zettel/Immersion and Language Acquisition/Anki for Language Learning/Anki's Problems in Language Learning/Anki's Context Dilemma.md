# Anki's Context Dilemma

## Claim

Anki has a context problem. A card can never convey the complete feel of a word. Even with images, audio, and cloze deletions, much of the lived context is missing: emotion, situation, and the minutes before and after.

Reviews become slow and overloaded; learners remember the initial context to derive the answer; and the word's connotation changes for them. They remember the card, context, translation, or learning moment instead of developing a genuine feel for the word.

Context should support meaning, not itself become the object being remembered.

## Why It Matters

This clarifies what a card should do, when Anki helps, and where immersion must perform the real work of building meaning.
