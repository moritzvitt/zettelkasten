# Anki and the Problem of Weighting Meanings

## Claim

Reviews, card design, and conscious attention can give some words or meanings more mental weight than they have in real language use. Anki can thereby distort the language's natural weighting, which would normally emerge from how often words are encountered.

This can slow acquisition: learners develop an unnatural feel for words, lack the words that fit actual situations, and over-fix individual meanings of broad, context-dependent words. With synonyms, Anki may assign artificially distinct colors even though their differences in real usage are gradual and contextual.

## Why It Matters

Anki works better for narrowly defined terms with stable meanings. The initial meaning of a new word should therefore remain minimal: one initial connotation, not a fully defined concept.
