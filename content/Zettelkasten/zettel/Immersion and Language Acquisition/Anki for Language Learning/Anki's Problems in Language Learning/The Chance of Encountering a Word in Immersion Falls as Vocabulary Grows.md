# The Chance of Encountering a Word in Immersion Falls as Vocabulary Grows

## Claim

The rarer the words being learned, the lower the chance of naturally encountering a newly learned word again soon.

The mental "hook" increasingly loses its function: reviews keep the word artificially active, but it receives hardly any genuine input context. The learner remembers the card, translation, or learning moment instead of developing a feel for the word.

Compensating would require either vastly more input or continually increasing Anki effort. Neither scales well. If a natural re-encounter is not likely enough, Anki often preserves an artificial memory rather than supporting acquisition.

## Why It Matters

This clarifies what a card should do, when Anki helps, and where immersion must perform the real work of building meaning.
