# Multilingualism Is Reactivation, Not Constant Performance

## Claim

Multilingualism does not mean keeping all languages equally active at all times. The real skill is being able to bring a language network back online quickly when needed.[^source-notes]

A language can therefore be "there" without being fluently available in the moment. What matters is not constant peak performance, but how quickly input, routines, and familiar contexts make the language accessible again.

## Why It Matters

This shifts the learning strategy from permanent maintenance to reactivation ability: short re-immersion, targeted listening, shadowing, context shifts, and interference control become more important.

That also makes learning Japanese part of the same logic. Not every component has to be trained maximally all the time, but the system must reliably restore access.

[^source-notes]: Source notes: Mehrsprachigkeit als Reaktivierung; Japanisch und Anki Backfill.
