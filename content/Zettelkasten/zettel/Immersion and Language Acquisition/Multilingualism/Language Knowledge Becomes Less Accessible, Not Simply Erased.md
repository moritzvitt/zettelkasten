## Claim

Unused language knowledge often becomes less accessible rather than simply being erased. Reactivation therefore means making existing traces reachable and fluent again.

## Why It Matters

A language does not need to be kept permanently at peak performance with Anki. Once deeply learned, it can later be reactivated through input, output, and targeted re-encounters.
