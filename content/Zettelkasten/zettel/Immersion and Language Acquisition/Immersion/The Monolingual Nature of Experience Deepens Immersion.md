# The Monolingual Nature of Experience Deepens Immersion

## Claim

Immersion becomes denser when the target language is not constantly routed through translation, but serves as the medium of understanding itself.

The learner searches, infers, misunderstands, corrects, and continues within the language. Meaning then connects directly to sounds, situations, images, and intentions rather than always passing through the native language.

## Why It Matters

Monolingual experience strengthens direct access and helps a language become an environment rather than merely an object of study.
