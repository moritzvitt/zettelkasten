# Comprehensibility vs Attention - Choosing Materials for Immersion

## Claim

Input lies in a field of tension between comprehensibility and attention.
*Drawing 2026-05-04 14.32.38.excalidraw – nicht öffentlich verfügbar*

Language acquisition begins with comprehensible input: learners must grasp enough meaning for the brain to extract patterns, forms, and probabilities from real utterances.[^input-output-immersion-sources]

The sweet spot lies where the content is slightly above the learner's current level.

> [!NOTE]
> Input is not effective merely as background exposure. It becomes especially useful when attention arises through the desire to understand, recognition, surprise, context, subtitles, lookups, attempts at output, or feedback.[^input-output-immersion-sources]

## Why It Matters

Suitable input follows a simple formula:
1. Interest in the content is very important. Effortless attention is the key; material that engages several senses is probably most effective.
2. The content should be somewhat above the learner's current level.
