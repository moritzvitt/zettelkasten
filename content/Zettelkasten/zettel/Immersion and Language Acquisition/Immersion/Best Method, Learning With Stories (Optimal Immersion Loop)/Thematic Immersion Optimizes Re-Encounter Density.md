# Thematic Immersion Optimizes Re-Encounter Density

## Claim

Thematic immersion optimizes the density of re-encounters. When several pieces of input share a genre, subject, setting, or format, relevant vocabulary and patterns recur more often than they would in randomly mixed input.

The repetition remains contextual rather than mechanical. The same expression appears with different speakers, situations, and nuances, allowing a richer semantic network to develop.

## Why It Matters

Thematic focus makes rarer vocabulary more learnable and reduces the amount of artificial repetition Anki must provide.
