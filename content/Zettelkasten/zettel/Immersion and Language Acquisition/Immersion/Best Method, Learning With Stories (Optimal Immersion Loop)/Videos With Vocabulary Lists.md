# Videos With Vocabulary Lists

## Claim

Videos with vocabulary lists connect study items to the exact medium in which they occurred.

## Why It Matters

A list makes expressions findable again without stripping them entirely from voice, scene, timing, and story. It can support repeated immersion without turning every word into an Anki card.
