# Beauty

## Claim

Beauty should not be thought about too much if one wants it to illuminate the soul.

When one encounters beauty, one may be happy about the moment and marvel at the [[zettelkasten/zettel/isn't-life-beautiful/axiom---the-wonders-of-this-world|Axiom - The Wonders of This World]].

Thinking too much about beauty can destroy it for oneself.

## Why It Matters

This note preserves a basic intuition that can serve as a horizon of value and meaning for other reflections in the zettelkasten.
