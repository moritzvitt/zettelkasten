## Claim

A Danish holding-company structure can organize ownership so that a company is not simply treated as freely liquidatable private property.

The interesting idea is that ownership can enable control without allowing the owner to extract all assets. This binds the company more strongly to an enduring purpose.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
