# Housing Is a Fundamental Right

## Claim

Housing is a fundamental right because without reliable housing, a person can hardly be free, secure, or politically capable of acting.

When housing is treated primarily as an investment asset, the logic of ownership conflicts with basic social security. A sustainable constitution must therefore clarify where property rights end and the basic conditions of a dignified life begin.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
