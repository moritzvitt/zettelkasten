# An Understanding of Nature and Sustainability Is Present Within Us

## Claim

An understanding of nature and sustainability does not have to be imposed entirely from outside; it can connect with existing human motives of protection, care, and meaning.

Institutions such as steward ownership or a Purpose GmbH thereby become interesting. They do not merely try to constrain greed, but to create structures in which long-term responsibility can take effect more easily.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
