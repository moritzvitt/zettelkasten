# Tax Unfairness

## Claim

Tax unfairness arises not only from high or low tax rates, but from unequal opportunities for avoidance.

When earned income is taxed relatively directly while large fortunes can be sheltered through foundations, corporate structures, inheritance rules, or valuation questions, the burden shifts. Tax policy then becomes a question of bargaining power and institutional access.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
