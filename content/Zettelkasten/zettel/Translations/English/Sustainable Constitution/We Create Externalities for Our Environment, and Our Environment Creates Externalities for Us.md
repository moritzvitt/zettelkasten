## Claim

Our actions affect the environment, but the environment also acts back upon us.

Externalities are therefore not a marginal problem, but the basic form of human embeddedness. Energy use, pollution, climate effects, and resource consumption return to us as living conditions, health risks, and political conflicts. Sustainability begins when this feedback is no longer suppressed.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
