# Extreme Wealth Concentration Is Also Ownership Concentration

## Claim

Extreme wealth concentration is also ownership concentration: a small number of people then control not only money, but enduring claims on housing, companies, land, infrastructure, and channels of political influence.

The question is therefore not merely whether someone owns a great deal. The deeper question is which areas of social decision-making are permanently controlled through this ownership and which people are thereby excluded from participation.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
