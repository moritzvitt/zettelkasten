## Claim

An enterprise foundation partially separates a company's purpose from the short-term extraction interests of individual owners.

It is an alternative to the idea that ownership primarily means the right to privatize profits and freely liquidate assets. It becomes interesting for a sustainable constitution because it can institutionally safeguard continuity, responsibility, and commitment to purpose.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
