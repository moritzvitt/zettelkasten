# Privatizing Profits While Society Bears the Externalities

## Claim

A central sustainability problem arises when profits are captured privately while ecological, social, or financial follow-up costs fall on society.

The market then fails to measure the full price of an action. A company appears profitable even though part of its costs has merely been displaced onto the environment, public health, future generations, taxpayers, or weaker contracting parties.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
