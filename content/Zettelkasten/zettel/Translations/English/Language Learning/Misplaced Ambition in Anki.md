## Claim

Anki should not learn the entire language. It prepares the actual learning by creating recognition, keeping words in a learnable state, and marking weak retrieval points.[^source-notes]

It should not perfectly teach every connotation, collect rare words without real context, eliminate all speaking uncertainty, or keep several languages artificially fresh forever. Anki is too slow, costly, and context-poor for that. ^a0d869

Put provocatively: Anki does not learn a language. It merely prepares the real learning. Obsidian is closer to a knowledge store; Anki is a retrieval and activation tool.

## Why It Matters

This boundary protects against misplaced ambition. Anki prepares access; it does not replace immersion, speaking, reading, listening, or a genuine knowledge system.

[^source-notes]: Source notes: Mehrsprachigkeit als Reaktivierung; Anki als Sprachlernwerkzeug.
