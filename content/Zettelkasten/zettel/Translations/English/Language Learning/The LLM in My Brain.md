## Claim

When we learn a language, our brain effectively builds an LLM for that language. This LLM is refined continuously through input and output.

## Why It Matters

This note helps frame immersion not as a mere quantity of input, but as a designed learning environment with attention, repeated encounters, and growing comprehensibility.
