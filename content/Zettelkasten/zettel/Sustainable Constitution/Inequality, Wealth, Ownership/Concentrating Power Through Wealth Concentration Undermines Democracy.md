## Claim

Extreme wealth concentration becomes politically dangerous as soon as it translates into lasting concentrations of power.

Democracy requires that public decisions are not permanently overshadowed by a few centers of private wealth. When ownership produces not only rights of use but also political influence, media power, lobbying access, and the ability to block institutions, formal equality is hollowed out in practice.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
