## Claim

Sustainability means living and conducting economic activity in a way that does not consume the foundations of that life.

A sustainable way of life must not permanently take more from the planet than can regenerate. It would have to align with Earth's energy and material cycles instead of purchasing present advantages through future harm.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
