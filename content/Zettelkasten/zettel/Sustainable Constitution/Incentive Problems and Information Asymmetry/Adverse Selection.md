# Adverse Selection

## Claim

Adverse selection describes a situation in which bad risks enter a system more frequently because good risks are harder to distinguish.

When rules do not reveal who causes long-term costs, responsible actors are not automatically rewarded. A market or institution may then attract precisely the structures that undermine sustainability most severely.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
