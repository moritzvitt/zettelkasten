# Incentive Problems

## Claim

Incentive problems arise when an institution rewards behavior that it should actually discourage.

This becomes especially visible in companies when ownership, management, and affected parties diverge: owners, managers, employees, creditors, and the environment do not bear the same consequences. A sustainable constitution must therefore do more than formulate good intentions. It must organize accountability so that harmful long-term behavior becomes less attractive.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
