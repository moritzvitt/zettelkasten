## Claim

Moral hazard arises when someone decides about a risk but does not personally bear its full consequences.

The problem is not merely individual misconduct, but a poor structure of accountability. When profits remain private while losses fall partly on creditors, employees, the environment, or the public, risky behavior becomes systematically more likely.

## Why It Matters

This note marks an institutional point at which a sustainable constitution would need to organize responsibility, ownership, power, or externalities more clearly.
