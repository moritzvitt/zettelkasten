# Axiom - The Dignity of Life Is Inviolable

## Claim

##

## Why It Matters

The first article of Germany's Basic Law does not go far enough. "Human dignity shall be inviolable" still places humanity at the center and disregards the interaction between humans and their environment.

At a time when the externalities of human action on the environment, and in turn on humanity, are becoming increasingly obvious, humanity must accept its responsibility as the protector of life on this planet.
