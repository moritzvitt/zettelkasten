# Beauty (don't optimise Beauty)

## Claim

Beauty should not be thought about too much if one wants it to illuminate the soul.

When one encounters beauty, one may be happy about the moment and marvel at the [[tea-garden/isn't-life-beautiful/axiom---the-wonders-of-this-world|Axiom - The Wonders of This World]].

Thinking too much about beauty can destroy it for oneself.
